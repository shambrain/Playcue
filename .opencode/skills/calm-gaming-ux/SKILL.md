Read docs/DESIGN_PRINCIPLES.md, VISUAL_DESIGN_SYSTEM.md, INTERACTION_AND_MICROCOPY.md and SCREEN_SPECIFICATIONS.md. Design for one-step frequent actions, progressive disclosure, recognition over recall, low clutter and user control. Playful means warm and expressive, not childish or noisy. No FOMO, streaks, guilt, forced social presence or default feed. Every suggestion is dismissible; every status is accurate; every important action has feedback. Respect reduced motion and quiet hours. Cite user research; test rather than asserting comfort.
